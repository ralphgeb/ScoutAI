How it works
At 6:00 each morning (London time) the app reads about 100 tech, finance, world and fashion news sites. A quick first pass drops junk and groups the same story from different outlets. Then an AI reads each story and writes a summary and key points in its own words. It flags rumours (including market-moving world news) with odds of being true, lists stocks the story could move, and writes the Today tech briefing.
Some story pictures are found with DuckDuckGo and Brave Search. Write-ups, odds and stock effects are AI-generated and can be wrong. Not financial advice. Always check the original article.
